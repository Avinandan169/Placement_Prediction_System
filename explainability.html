Methodological Limitations & Model Constraints
Critical Reflection
  • Correlation vs Causation: High regression coefficients reflect predictive correlations in historical campus placements; attending placement training correlates with high recruitment success because motivated students actively seek out training.
  • Multicollinearity: School performance (SSC and HSC) correlates moderately with university CGPA (\(r \approx 0.35 - 0.45\)), which splits linear coefficient magnitudes among academic variables.
  • Dataset Context: Models trained on external benchmark datasets (such as Krooz Campus Recruitment) provide generalized patterns and must be fine-tuned or re-evaluated when applied to distinct regional institutional cohorts.
